# Project Armageddon Weapon Design v2.0

## Overview
This document proposes 10 new weapons for ProjectArmageddon's 2D artillery browser game, expanding the arsenal with advanced mechanics and diverse damage types.

## Weapon Categories & Distribution
- **Heavy Ranged (3)**: Plasma Cannon, Cryo Cannon, Solar Flare - Ultimate weapons with high damage and area effects
- **Ranged (4)**: Graviton Anchor, Neural Disruptor, Acid Sprayer, Biomass Spitter - Tactical weapons with unique elemental effects
- **Melee (3)**: Quantum Gauntlet, Arc Blade, Void Blade - High-damage close combat weapons with special abilities

## Damage Type Diversity
- **Energy**: Plasma Cannon, Quantum Gauntlet, Neural Disruptor, Arc Blade
- **Physical**: Graviton Anchor, Void Blade, traditional melee weapons
- **Ice**: Cryo Cannon - freezing and terrain manipulation
- **Fire**: Solar Flare - explosive area damage
- **Poison**: Acid Sprayer, Biomass Spitter - damage over time
- **Void**: Void Blade - unique corruption mechanic

## New Mechanics Introduced
1. **Piercing System** - Plasma Cannon can pierce 3 enemies
2. **Gravity Wells** - Pull enemies toward explosion
3. **EMP Stun** - Disable electronics and stun enemies
4. **Lightning Chain** - Chain damage between nearby targets
5. **Void Corruption** - Drain health/armor from enemies and terrain
6. **Timed Explosions** - Biomass Spitter and Cryo Cannon have duration effects

## Balance Philosophy
- **Rarity Scaling**: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary
- **Ammo Management**: Higher rarity weapons have reduced ammo capacity
- **Cooldown Management**: Powerful weapons require longer recovery times
- **Energy Systems**: New weapons introduce elemental resistance/weakness systems

## Implementation Notes
- All weapons use existing icon asset IDs (weapon_icon_046-055)
- New mechanics require minimal engine modifications
- Balance parameters can be tuned based on gameplay testing
- Energy types enable future elemental resistance/weakness systems
